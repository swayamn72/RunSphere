# Mascot storyline and character guide

Status: working fiction for the RunSphere crew. Characters are calm guides, not
authorities, reward-givers, or rejection signals (see
[`mascot-assets.md`](mascot-assets.md) and `src/mascot.ts`).

## The story

RunSphere is the network of trails, paths, and green corridors that thread an
ordinary city into something explorable. When a person heads outdoors - walking,
running, or hiking, at _their_ pace - the trail briefly wakes a **mover**: a
small spirit that keeps them company, but never judges their speed.

Four movers accompany the loop:

- **Loop** (the in-app guide) is the quiet orbit - the compass. It orients,
  points north, and lights the next step. It never celebrates a rank or pushes a
  pace.
- **Rho** is the rhythm of the daily mile - the steady pulse of showing up and
  moving.
- **Mira** is the scout - the one who finds the checkpoint, the detour, the
  interesting thing around the next corner.
- **Coda** is the connector - the relay partner who makes a solo route a shared
  one.
- **Bram** is the trail guardian - the one who holds the neighbourhood and its
  seasons.

Together they take a person from a single step (Rho), through discovery (Mira),
to company (Coda) and belonging (Bram), with Loop quietly guiding the whole way.
That maps to the product sequence: **daily movement -> quests ->
friends/challenges/clubs -> territory** - no pace, no comparison, no pressure.

## Style anchor

Shared visual rule for every character (used to keep a hand-made set consistent,
and reused as the prompt prefix if artwork is generated elsewhere):

> Flat vector mascot illustration for an adult outdoor-fitness app. Rounded,
> softly-geometric characters with a single bold outline. Restricted palette:
> sage greens, teal, forest, warm sand-cream, and a single lime accent. Matte
> shading, soft shadow under feet, no photorealism, no text, no logos, no
> hatching. Friendly and calm, adult not babyish, no aggressive features.
> Consistent line weight and palette across the entire set.

## Image prompts

Each prompt is intended to be pasted with the style anchor above. These describe
what the character _is_ so artwork can be produced consistently; they are not
product requirements beyond the guardrails in `mascot-assets.md`.

1. **Rho - the steady mover.** A compact, rounded water-bear-like creature with a
   soft rounded body, two small dot eyes, and short steady feet. A small glowing
   lime heartbeat/rhythm pulse drawn across its chest. Standing in a calm
   ready-to-step pose, one foot slightly forward, with a thin curved trail-path
   marking along its back. Represents showing up and moving at your own pace.
2. **Mira - the scout.** A slightly taller, angular scout creature with a long
   thin neck and a tiny backpack. A rolled map tucked under one arm and a single
   lime beacon dot floating above a raised hand like a compass star. Head tilted
   upward, looking eagerly toward a distant path. Represents discovery.
3. **Coda - the connector.** A rounded pair-bond companion creature holding a
   short relay baton loosely in one paw. Two tiny connection sparks or antennae
   forming a gentle arc over its head. Open, welcoming posture with a small
   friend-spark motif near its chest. Represents turning a solo route into a
   shared one.
4. **Bram - the trail guardian.** A sturdier, rocky trail-guardian creature
   wearing a peaked trail-hat hood. A small stylized mountain/peak ridge along
   its back and a single lime compass star on its chest. Standing planted on two
   broad feet, calm and watchful. Represents the neighbourhood, its trails, and
   its seasons.
